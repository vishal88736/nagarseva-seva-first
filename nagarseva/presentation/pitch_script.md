# NagarSeva — three-minute presentation

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem and promise

“A home is useful when the household can afford living there and basic services actually work. NagarSeva helps cities compare housing improvements and verify service delivery through resident feedback and recorded evidence.”

## 0:25–0:55 — One project

“Here is our selected project. Its planning profile is clearly labelled synthetic. Every part of the app carries the same project: priority, finances, household costs, service records and complaints. Approved pilot locations can be imported separately and remain unassessed until evidence or an approved review score is supplied.”

Show the map/profile and downloadable project brief. Explain that feature signal bars are not SHAP attribution percentages and synthetic ranking accuracy is agreement with a constructed policy formula.

## 0:55–1:20 — Affordable for whom?

“The financial screen makes area, rehab assumptions, market rates and costs inspectable. Beside developer viability, we compare household rent or EMI, maintenance, utilities, transport and income loss. Temporary rent and moving expenses stay visible separately. All starting values are editable illustrative assumptions.”

Change the household income or one cost and download the scenario. The user-set planning limit is not an official lending or eligibility threshold.

## 1:20–2:20 — Prove the service workflow

“This resident report is a labelled demo. It has a permanent server record and private tracking receipt. An officer reviews the suggested category and urgency, assigns responsibility and records the repair measurement, method, date and evidence. The service score waits for the resident's response. Confirmation accepts the reading; dispute reopens the complaint and excludes the disputed measurement.”

Show one prepared synthetic complaint in each stage. Do not describe a simulated repair as a real intervention. Show the updated service record and its verification label. Resident confirmation is distinct from a field reviewer action and government certification.

## 2:20–2:45 — Credible comparisons and evidence

“Allocations enforce budget, vulnerability proxy and distinct ward coverage. An equal-ward baseline is computed independently; infeasibility and unused budget are visible. The pilot workspace records source-backed commitments, consented observations, timings, feedback and multilingual classification errors. Real-pilot metrics exclude demo records and start empty.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Pilot request

“We are seeking one approved project or ward, a resident/NGO partner and a municipal reviewer. We will measure usability, response and verified service outcomes before expanding. Our working demonstration is complete; a field pilot remains the next evidence-gathering step.”

## Questions to prepare for

- **What is real?** The software and persistent workflow are implemented. Map background geography is OpenStreetMap; area forecasts come from Open-Meteo with retrieval times. Baseline settlement markers/features/model labels remain synthetic. Pilot records have explicit sources; no field pilot is claimed.
- **Why another dashboard?** The project links housing/household trade-offs to an operational complaint, evidence and resident confirmation trail. Official service portals are linked for handoff; NagarSeva does not claim to submit reports to them.
- **Does AI decide eligibility or relocation?** No. It supports synthetic planning review. PMAY-U 2.0 screening links to current official guidance and requires authority verification; imported locations do not receive synthetic intervention probabilities.
- **Can someone close a resident's complaint?** Officer completion requests confirmation. The resident's private receipt is required to confirm or dispute. Receipt tokens are hashed in storage.
- **Does a photo prove water quality?** No. Evidence has a method, unit, date and reviewer state. The current water measure is availability in hours/day, not potability certification.
- **What happens offline?** Planning previews and financial calculations are labelled offline. Operational writes report failure and preserve the form; drafts are explicitly unsent.
- **How will this be used?** Local demo now, then an approved small pilot with officer access and consent. Production identity, retention governance, integrations and independent validation remain deployment work.

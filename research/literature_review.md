This is a focused decision review, not an exhaustive systematic review or a meta-analysis. Publication dates below come from publication/repository records; search-engine crawl dates were ignored.

## Synthesis for the build decision

The reviewed evidence supports three design requirements: preserve resident participation and livelihood information; measure actual service performance separately from assets; and expose the assumptions and uncertainty behind comparisons. These are **research-derived requirements**, not evidence that one new product is absent everywhere.

Local surveys, community GIS, scorecards and integrated mapping frameworks already exist. The defensible contribution to investigate is a small, reproducible workflow that links dated sources and service observations, surfaces missing evidence, and tests whether an officer/NGO can reach a better-supported decision faster. Neither the literature nor the desk research establishes willingness to adopt, current local failure rates or project impact.

## Source-use cautions

- Historical claims about notification and water entitlement in L03/L04 must be read with the later [BMC Water for All policy reported from May 2022](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/HomePage%20Data/Whats%20New/English%20Year%20Book%202025%20Portal.pdf).
- A review's reported classification accuracy belongs to its data and protocol. No accuracy, income effect, health gain or repair-time reduction has been reproduced by this project.
- National policy evaluations are not evidence of conditions in a chosen ward. Qualitative resident experiences establish important mechanisms, without establishing population prevalence.
- Open reading does not automatically permit copying training imagery, republishing household surveys or redistributing an entire publication. Inspect the exact asset's licence.

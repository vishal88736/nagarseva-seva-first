# Existing systems and the duplication test

Access date: **17 October 2026**. Portal access results are recorded in [official_sources.md](official_sources.md). “Documented” means an official specification or institution's description; “observed” means material actually returned during this research. Neither implies that a protected workflow was successfully used.

## Policy fit and responsibility

The [Maharashtra Slum Areas Act official text as on 6 February 2026](https://lj.maharashtra.gov.in/en/document/the-maharashtra-slum-areas-improvement-clearance-and-redevelopment-act-1971-text-as-on-6th-february-2026/) provides the statutory context for slum authorities, improvement and redevelopment. The [Mumbai DCPR 2034 base document](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/MCGM%20Department%20List/Chief%20Engineer%20%28Development%20Plan%29/Docs/SANCTIONED%20DP2034/DCPR/DCPR%202034.pdf) includes regulation 33(10). Later amendments, project-specific permissions, land ownership and applicable authority require legal/official checking. These documents do not authorise software to determine entitlement or relocation.

Proposed Mumbai adoption would need **BMC's M/E ward administration with the relevant service department** as an operational sponsor. SRA would be a source/partner for applicable rehabilitation-project information, rather than the assumed repair authority for every civic asset. An actual CBO/operator agreement may assign daily maintenance: BMC describes such arrangements under its sanitation programme. The relevant MoU and delegated authority must be inspected for the selected site. [BMC Year Book 2025](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/HomePage%20Data/Whats%20New/English%20Year%20Book%202025%20Portal.pdf)

## Questions that would establish an incremental contribution

Before building beyond a desk demonstration, obtain a walkthrough of one real inspection or review. Ask the officer/operator to show: the authoritative asset ID; current source of operational status; evidence accepted for closure; records already exchanged between systems; and the next decision that is delayed by missing or contradictory evidence. Request a de-identified example, rather than credentials or resident lists.

If an existing report already answers the decision adequately, configure or export from it. If evidence cannot legally be shared, use public aggregate records and reduce the claim. **No absence of a feature has been established solely from an inaccessible portal or its landing page.**

# Conditional pilot and adoption path

Prepared 18 October 2026. Proposed sponsor: BMC M-East ward administration. Proposed water-domain review owner: Assistant Engineer (Water Works), M-East, with the actual asset operator. The [current department list](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/portal/anonymous/qlacmeward) lists this role. The [pilot overview](../pilot/README.md) records the published manual and its limitations. No office, site or operator has agreed.

| Window | Proposed work | Measurable gate |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Agree one inspection/closure task, lawful records, current area and ownership; observe existing process | Written owner/permission and a preregistered independent comparator; otherwise pause operational work |
| Days 31–60 | Prepare up to 20 public water assets and aim for three dated review rounds; preserve missed visits and disagreement | Report actual assets, observations and source completeness. Proposed 60 observations is not an achieved count |
| Days 61–90 | Compare current-process and prototype briefs with counterbalanced tasks; validate rates and handover | Actual task time, critical omissions and reviewer feedback; documented continue/adapt/stop decision |

The [field protocol](../pilot/field_protocol.md) distinguishes a functioning point at one visit from daily supply hours and potability. Do not enter a spot check as hours/day. The [indicator protocol](../pilot/indicator_protocol.md) specifies missingness and the approvals needed before real composite scoring.

## Responsibilities before operation

The host approves use, access, retention and publication. The domain officer defines service targets and review/closure rules. The asset operator owns the agreed maintenance response. An independent reviewer records the comparison and field checks. Residents provide voluntary accessible feedback; participation must not determine benefits or tenure. The applicant maintains the trial software under a scoped agreement. Each role and recurring payer must be named in the actual trial agreement.

Start with approved files and existing complaint references. The prototype's issue form is a demonstration, not an alternative official grievance channel. No live SRA, MHADA, PMAY or BMC integration is promised.

## Conditional scale

At six months, test transfer to a second task/site only if the initial comparison shows useful results. At twelve months, consider supported handover with approved roles and finance. Over three to five years, expand only after independent transfer checks, permitted data sharing, accessible language support, security assessment and recurring budgets. No city-wide beneficiary or revenue forecast is available.

Stop if no lawful input, accountable owner, independent comparator or practical advantage can be established. Asset repair and housing construction need separate funding and engineering/statutory approval.

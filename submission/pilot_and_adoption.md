# Proposed pilot and government adoption

Prepared **17 October 2026**. All targets below are proposed trial design, not measured results or government commitments.

## Decision and owner

**Decision:** which public sanitation assets in one agreed M/E cluster require the next inspection, and what evidence supports that referral?

**Prospective sponsor:** BMC M/E ward administration, led by the competent ward officer together with the relevant sanitation/service department. The department and actual CBO/contract operator must confirm maintenance responsibility for each asset. SRA/MHADA supply applicable scheme context. No office has endorsed this proposal. BMC's descriptions of sanitation maintenance arrangements are background, not the contract for the selected asset. [BMC Year Book 2025](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/HomePage%20Data/Whats%20New/English%20Year%20Book%202025%20Portal.pdf), [SRA records](https://dms.sra.gov.in/SRAPUBLIC/)

Request a **bounded review trial**, rather than replacement of municipal GIS or a new grievance portal. Use existing asset/complaint identifiers where authorised. Existing SBM monitoring and Swachhata confirmation/reopening are prior art. [SBM Chapter 10](https://mahadma.maharashtra.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/swachh-bharat-2.pdf), [Swachhata description](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2162194&lang=2&reg=48)

## Proposed 30–60–90 day gates

| Window | Work and proposed deliverable | Proposed measurement / stop rule |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Confirm sponsor/operator, one current boundary and data-sharing terms. Register up to **20 public assets** with source IDs. Observe the existing review workflow. Complete consented interviews with a proposed **6–10 residents**, **2 staff/operators** and **1 community/NGO representative**, if available. | Baseline task time and critical evidence omissions recorded before showing the tool. No operational trial without lawful data, named owner and a safe observation protocol. These sample sizes are design targets. |
| Days 31–60 | Conduct **three observation rounds** over the 20 agreed assets, aiming for **60 dated asset observations**. Preserve operator claims and independent observations separately. Produce an inspection brief and compare it with the current process on identical cases. | Report actual attempted/completed visits and access failures. Blind the independent reviewer to tool ranking. No photo alone certifies water quality, structural safety or reliable operation. |
| Days 61–90 | Resolve record crosswalks with the authority, audit disagreements and assess review usability, time and maintenance effort. Provide data dictionary, export and handover guide. | Continue only if critical source omissions do not increase, uncertainty remains visible, users can reproduce selected conclusions and a responsible office accepts the workflow and recurring budget. Time savings and agreement thresholds must be agreed before analysis, not retrofitted. |

The proposed sample is a feasibility exercise. It cannot establish ward-wide prevalence, causal health impact or statistical representativeness. Emergencies follow the authority's existing escalation pathway; this prototype does not receive emergency complaints.

## Responsibilities to negotiate

| Actor | Proposed responsibility | Required confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Ward sponsor | Approve scope, appoint review owner and resolve jurisdiction disputes | Written trial authorisation and named official post |
| Service department / operator | Provide permitted asset IDs, relevant standard, maintenance duties and existing records | Applicable contract/MoU and workflow |
| Resident/community representative | Advise on access, opening hours, fees, disability barriers and exclusion; voluntary feedback | Consent process and accessible participation |
| Individual developer | Maintain local prototype, document transformations and fix defects | Scope, support capacity and code/licence review |
| Independent reviewer | Check records and same-window observations; record unresolved disagreements | Conflict declaration and blinded review protocol |
| BMC data/security owner | Define hosting, access, retention and publication rules before live deployment | Approved controls; current local prototype has no shared-case security system |

## Adoption handover pack

Provide the original input/version register, authorised crosswalks, reproducible analysis settings, unresolved evidence list, operator responsibility register, accessibility checks, cost schedule and export examples. Start with an approved file export. Promise no SRA/MHADA/Aaple Sarkar API integration until the owning agency confirms an available interface and permission.

Funding options are an approved innovation work order or grant for the trial, a permitted CSR contribution for independent community work, and an approved departmental operating allocation for continuation. None is committed. PPP or land-value capture finances wider physical projects and is disproportionate to this evidence-review prototype. No beneficiary payment or microfinance is proposed for the digital trial. Actual asset repairs need their own engineering estimates and procurement route.

## Conditional 3–5 year path

Year 1: validate one workflow and stable IDs in one ward. Year 2: test transfer to a second ward/operator, including Marathi and assisted use. Year 3: integrate only approved exports/APIs and independent audits. Years 4–5: expand by approved service/ULB after recurring budget, interoperability, security and resident inclusion are demonstrated. These are decision gates, not adoption forecasts or beneficiary projections.

## Material risks

| Risk | Mitigation / stop condition |
|---|---|
| Existing system already supplies an adequate brief | Compare the actual export and staff task first; improve that export or stop the product proposal. |
| Shared CTS/locality causes a false match | Preserve original IDs, context and candidate matches; require authority review before any crosswalk. |
| Missing records look like deprivation | Show unknowns and observation dates. Publish no current household rate without its proper survey denominator. |
| Reprisals or sensitive household information | Collect aggregate asset evidence, minimise personal details and suppress identifying free text. Participation is voluntary. |
| Operator marks a repair complete without a sustained service check | Preserve claim and independent observation separately; inspect repeat failures through the existing process. |
| Solo support capacity is inadequate | Bounded trial, documented handover and an agreed maintenance owner before expansion. |

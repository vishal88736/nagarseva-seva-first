# UrbanSaarthi validation materials

Prepared 18 October 2026. The [pilot directory](../pilot/README.md) contains the unsent office request, observation and baseline protocol, indicator contract and blank quote/assessment templates. No interviews, quotations or independent labels were invented.

Use these materials in order: confirm the task and operator, get data permission, freeze the comparator rubric before exposing engine scores, gather approved observations, compare paired reviews, then decide whether to continue. Preserve refusals, missed visits and negative findings. The applicant must privately complete contact details before sending the draft request through the normal administrative route.

Software checks remain separately recorded in [application CI](../integration/ci-validation.json) and [actual Chromium results](../integration/urban-validation.json). Sensitivity on the synthetic fixture is available now. Independent priority agreement and field-service accuracy remain unavailable until real assessments exist.

# Workflow and resident validation kit

Status: **prepared, not conducted**. No responses, interviews or endorsements are claimed. Use only after the participant/authority agrees. No outreach has been sent.

## Invitation draft

I am preparing an individual research prototype for the Seva First Innovation Challenge. It organises dated public records and service observations for an inspection review. I would like a short walkthrough of how you currently review one service issue and feedback on an example evidence brief. This is a research discussion, not an official complaint or promise of assistance. Please use public or de-identified examples. Participation is voluntary. May I take anonymous notes? I will not record audio, collect identity documents or publish identifying remarks without separate consent.

Do not send this automatically. The applicant chooses the recipient and obtains consent.

## Officer/operator walkthrough, approximately 25 minutes

1. Which actual decision do you make, and which office/operator must act?
2. Show one permitted completed review. Which IDs, dates, standards and evidence were necessary?
3. Which existing MIS/GIS/export or complaint channel already answers this question?
4. Where did the reviewer need clarification? Record an example before discussing the proposed product.
5. What evidence would change the next inspection or acceptance of a repair?
6. How are contractor claims, resident reports and disagreements recorded today?
7. Which information may this prototype lawfully receive, and what must stay restricted?
8. Would a dated file export fit the existing process? Who would maintain it and pay recurring costs?

Record anonymised role, date, task, existing tool, elapsed time, omitted evidence, unresolved issues and the person's exact proposed change. Record refusal or an adequate existing process as meaningful negative evidence.

## Resident discussion, approximately 15 minutes

Use an accessible location/language and a facilitator the participant accepts. Include women, older residents, disabled residents, renters and migrants voluntarily, without collecting eligibility labels.

- Which public service do you use, at what usual times, and what makes access difficult?
- Are queues, fees, safety, opening times, water availability or physical barriers relevant?
- What happened the last time the service failed, and which existing channel did you try?
- Does the sample brief reflect the issue correctly? What would you remove or keep private?
- How could someone without a smartphone review or challenge this record?

Do not infer service reliability from a single visit. Do not ask for Aadhaar, bank details, legal status, household income or health records. Do not promise housing entitlement or repairs.

## Small comparative task protocol

Choose 6–10 permitted cases with an independent reviewer defining required evidence before seeing the prototype. Have willing staff perform the same review using their normal method and the evidence brief. Counterbalance order across cases to reduce learning effects. Record start/stop rules, failures and assistance. Compare paired task time, critical-source omissions, correct identification of unknowns/conflicts and the user's decision explanation. Report the actual small sample, distribution and order effects. Do not convert this exercise into a causal ward-wide impact claim.

| Local trial case ID, public asset ID and boundary version | To record after authorisation |
| Observation date/time and permitted evidence reference | To record |
| Service dimension and applicable standard | To agree before observation |
| Evidence type | Official record / operator claim / independent observation / resident report |
| Observed value and uncertainty | Working / not working / unknown, with limited scope |
| Access barrier or failed visit | Record explicitly |
| Conflicting claim and follow-up owner | Preserve both references |
| Consent / publication restrictions | Record separately from public asset evidence |

Avoid collecting names in the prototype. Restricted consent records, if required, need an approved storage and retention process outside this public demo.

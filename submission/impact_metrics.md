# Proposed impact and evaluation register

All operational baselines are **unmeasured**. Targets are proposed trial criteria, not achieved performance. Engineering results are in [the executed report](../research/prototype_validation.md); the two evidence layers must remain separate.

| KPI / unit | Baseline | Proposed target or decision rule | Measurement and dependency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Records with source URL, source type and observation date or explicit unknown | Current workflow not measured | 100% of trial records preserve these fields | Schema audit over the actual trial extract; a supplied source is not independently verified truth |
| Incorrect automatic scheme merges | Not measured in existing workflow; prototype makes none in the selected case | Zero automatic merges from CTS/locality alone | Compare retained original keys and an authority-reviewed crosswalk; evaluate additional independently selected records |
| Critical evidence omissions per reviewed case | Not measured | No increase relative to the current process; agree acceptance before comparison | Independent reviewer defines essential evidence before seeing outputs, then checks identical cases |
| Median review time and assistance per case | Not measured | Improvement target to agree after baseline and before comparative trial | Counterbalanced paired task timing, failures and assistance. Small sample and order effects reported |
| Independently assessable service observations | No current local survey | Proposed 20 assets, three rounds, up to 60 observations | Report actual successful/failed visits and unknown values; observations cluster within assets |
| Claim disagreement and independently adjudicated agreement | Not measured | Report every disagreement; no unverified closure certification | Compare the same asset/service/time window. Independent evidence required, not a model's confidence |
| Candidate walking distance accuracy | No real network acquired | Inspect every published trial route; withhold inadequately mapped cases | Lawful network, entrances, restrictions and independent route checks. Proximity does not establish usability or hours |
| Repeat failures / service availability in agreed periods | Not measured | Predefine the service standard and repeat-observation protocol with operator | Actual logs and consented observations; one photograph cannot establish continuous uptime or water quality |
| Inclusion of access barriers | No participation performed | Offer assisted review and voluntarily seek diverse experiences | Record actual participation and barriers without sensitive eligibility labels; no representation claim from a convenience sample |
| Household benefit and cost per household | Verified denominator absent | Withhold until beneficiary/outcome definition and independent counts exist | Separate asset counts, observations, residents and households. Workbook denominator starts blank |
| Annual operating cost and accountable owner | Rates illustrative; owner unconfirmed | Obtain quotations, written operating scope and a recurring payer before expansion | Actual resource schedule, contract/MoU and funding approval |

The trial supports SDG 6's concern with usable water/sanitation, SDG 11's inclusive urban services and SDG 1's deprivation lens. This is alignment, not measured progress against official SDG indicators. No current household poverty, rights or eligibility attribute is inferred from imagery or a score.

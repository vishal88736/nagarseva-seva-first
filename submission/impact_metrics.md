# Impact and measurement register

Prepared 18 October 2026. Targets below are proposed trial actions, not measured improvements. The current service and resident-benefit baseline is unavailable.

| KPI | Baseline | Trial target / decision | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source completeness | Not measured on permitted operational records | Report actual completeness and exclusions | Required-field audit with source/date/geography/permission |
| Priority agreement | Independent labels not collected | Measure; no invented accuracy target | Hidden-score independent rankings, ties, n and missing pairs |
| Ranking sensitivity | Available for synthetic fixture | Expose changes under agreed weight alternatives | Rank changes and tie-aware Spearman, labelled sensitivity |
| Review quality | Existing-process omissions unmeasured | Predefine useful difference with owner | Independent assessment of critical omissions on paired cases |
| Review effort | No users observed | Measure both methods with counterbalanced order | Minutes per same defined task, actual n |
| Service functionality | No field observations | Aim for up to 20 assets and three rounds | Dated permitted measurements, failed visits and disagreement |
| Verified service reliability | Unmeasured | Repeat checks appropriate to the service | Valid observation windows; completion is separate from service functioning |
| Travel/access | Synthetic graph only | Validate real permitted entrances/network before reporting | Route audits including access restrictions, hours and disability barriers |
| Cost and coverage | Hypothetical inputs | Obtain engineering-reviewed quantities/rates | Quotes, dates, taxes, exclusions and checked arithmetic |
| Household benefit / SROI | Unknown | Withhold until denominator and attributable outcomes exist | Independent household/benefit design with permission |

Inclusion review must cover voluntary assisted participation, women, older and disabled residents, renters and migrants. Distance alone omits safety, fees, opening hours and usability. Intended SDG alignment: 6 (water/sanitation), 11 (inclusive settlements/services), 1 (deprivation). No SDG progress or population outcome is measured.
